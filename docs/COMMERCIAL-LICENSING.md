# Commercial Licensing

SlickLab Commerce is offered publicly as a source-available preview for evaluation and demonstration.

A separate written commercial license is required for production deployment, SaaS or hosted use, customer-facing commercial deployment, resale, redistribution, white-labeling, sublicensing, or commercial derivative products based on proprietary SlickLab Commerce material.

For a commercial license, contact the repository owner through the business contact published on the GitHub profile or repository once configured.

Final commercial agreements should specify scope, deployments, users or customers, support, updates, term, fees, IP rights, termination, warranties, liability, and applicable law.
